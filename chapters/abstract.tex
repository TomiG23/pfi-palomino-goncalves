\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

Argentine e-commerce is experiencing sustained growth, yet small-scale online store owners lack analytical tools that are simultaneously accessible, integrated, and prescriptive. Available solutions are limited to descriptive reports that record past events without indicating what action to take; international platforms with prescriptive capabilities present cost, language, and market-fit barriers that make them inaccessible to this segment.

This work describes the design, implementation, and evaluation of Decidata, a prototype system for actionable recommendations in e-commerce stores. The system integrates natively into the Tiendanube store administration panel and, through a multi-platform adapter, into WooCommerce as well. Based on each store's historical sales data, the analysis engine applies simple exponential smoothing and Holt-Winters for demand forecasting, RFM customer segmentation with k-means clustering, and a multi-armed bandit with Thompson Sampling to select and prioritize the recommendations with the highest expected impact. Recommendations are phrased in natural language using Google's Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite model and are differentiated according to each store's commercial segment: gastronomy, clothing, or electronics.

User research was conducted through five in-depth interviews with e-commerce entrepreneurs and a brainstorming session with technical, product and marketing profiles, including the owner of an online store. The process followed a Design Thinking methodology to translate the identified needs into user stories and formal requirements.

The prototype includes automated unit and integration tests for both the backend and the frontend, and was evaluated using script-generated data for three demonstration stores, one per commercial segment. A strategic analysis and an economic-financial viability study complete the proposal, identifying a market gap and indicators of willingness to pay in the target segment.

\bigskip
\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} e-commerce, prescriptive recommendations, demand forecasting, customer segmentation, multi-armed bandit, Tiendanube, WooCommerce, artificial intelligence.
