\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

Argentine e-commerce is experiencing sustained growth, yet small-scale online store owners lack analytical tools that are simultaneously accessible, integrated, and prescriptive. Available solutions are limited to descriptive reports that do not indicate what action to take; international platforms with prescriptive capabilities present cost, language, and market-fit barriers that make them inaccessible to this segment.

This work describes the design, implementation, and evaluation of Decidata, a prototype system for actionable recommendations in e-commerce stores. It integrates into the Tiendanube administration panel and, through a multi-platform adapter, into WooCommerce. Based on each store's historical sales data, the analysis engine applies simple exponential smoothing and Holt-Winters for demand forecasting, RFM customer segmentation with k-means clustering, and a multi-armed bandit with Thompson Sampling to prioritize the recommendations with the highest expected impact. Recommendations are phrased in natural language with Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite and are differentiated according to each store's commercial segment: gastronomy, clothing, or electronics. % PENDIENTE-IMPL: B1
Beyond recommending, it prepares the action, such as a coupon or a sale price, for the owner to confirm and apply with one click.

User research was conducted through five in-depth interviews with e-commerce entrepreneurs and a brainstorming session with technical, product and marketing profiles, including the owner of an online store. Design Thinking translated the identified needs into user stories and formal requirements.

The prototype includes automated tests for the backend and the frontend, and was evaluated using script-generated data for three demonstration stores, one per commercial segment. A strategic analysis and an economic-financial viability study identify a market gap and indicators of willingness to pay in the target segment.

\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} e-commerce, prescriptive recommendations, demand forecasting, customer segmentation, multi-armed bandit, Tiendanube, WooCommerce, artificial intelligence.
